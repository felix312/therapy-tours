Before you book

How far ahead should I book?

For the July to October migration and the December holidays, book four to six months ahead. In other months, four to eight weeks is usually enough.

Do I need a visa for Kenya?

Most visitors need an online travel authorization before arrival. Check the official Kenyan government site for your nationality, and we will send a confirmation letter with your booking.

Which vaccinations do I need?

Requirements depend on where you travel from. Ask a travel doctor four to six weeks before departure, including about malaria prevention.

Can you build a private itinerary?

Yes. Send your dates, group size and must-see places, and we will reply with a proposal within a day.

What is included in the price?

Full board on safari, a 4x4 Landcruiser with an English-speaking driver/guide, game drives and park fees. Flights, the Kenya eTA, insurance and optional activities are not included. Each itinerary lists the details.

Are children welcome?

Yes. We match families with camps that have shorter game drives and activities for younger guests.
