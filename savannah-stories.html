Savannah Stories: Amboseli, Nyahururu, Nakuru and Mara

Premium camps6 days

Day by day

  1. Day 1 · Tulia AmboseliCollected at the airport and driven toward Kilimanjaro. Lunch, then a first game drive in Amboseli.
  2. Day 2 · Tulia AmboseliMorning and late-afternoon game drives. All meals included.
  3. Day 3 · The CliffDrive to Thompson Falls at Nyahururu with lunch in a restaurant overlooking the falls, then on to the Lake Nakuru region for an afternoon game drive.
  4. Day 4 · Entim Main Camp / IlkelianiEarly game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park, with white rhino and buffalo almost guaranteed. After breakfast, drive to the Maasai Mara for lunch and an afternoon game drive.
  5. Day 5 · Entim Main Camp / IlkelianiMorning and afternoon game drives, or an optional full-day drive with picnic lunch. All meals included.
  6. Day 6 · DepartAfter breakfast, drive to Nairobi and transfer to your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.

Good to know

  • Entim Camp and Ilkeliani include curated spirits, house wines, beers and soft drinks, plus laundry (except underwear). At Ilkeliani, one child under 5 per tent stays free.
  • Easter weekend: 26–29 Mar 2027. Christmas and New Year: 22 Dec 2027 – 2 Jan 2028.
  • Rates are per adult sharing. Single room supplement shown in the last column.
