Unwind: Naivasha, Nakuru and Maasai Mara

Rift Valley + Mara6 days

Day by day

  1. Day 1 · Eka HotelMeet our representative at the hotel or airport and transfer to the hotel. No meals included.
  2. Day 2 · Great Rift Valley LodgeDrive to Naivasha for lunch at the lodge, then a one-hour boat ride with a visit to Crescent Island.
  3. Day 3 · Lake Nakuru LodgeDrive into Lake Nakuru National Park with a game drive on the way to the lodge. Lunch, then an afternoon game drive.
  4. Day 4 · Loyk Mara CampTravel to the Maasai Mara, arriving for lunch, followed by an afternoon game drive.
  5. Day 5 · Loyk Mara CampMorning and late afternoon game drives, or an optional full-day drive with picnic lunch.
  6. Day 6 · DepartAfter breakfast, drive back to Nairobi and transfer to your hotel or the airport.

Good to know

  • Loyk Mara Camp includes a guided nature walk.
  • Easter weekend: 26–29 Mar 2027. Christmas and New Year: 22 Dec 2027 – 2 Jan 2028.
  • Christmas supplement: US$35 adult, US$20 child.
  • Lake Nakuru Lodge: high-season rate applies at Easter.
