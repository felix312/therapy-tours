Privacy Policy

Information we collect

Through the website

  • Email address and telephone number.

By the company

  • Full names, identification details, telephone numbers and email addresses.
  • Nationality and passport information where necessary.
  • Emergency contact details.
  • Information about special travel requirements, including dietary and accessibility needs.

Booking and travel information

  • Destination and travel dates.
  • Number of travellers and room preferences.
  • Accommodation and transportation preferences.
  • Flight details and itinerary information.
  • Safari preferences, excursions and activities.
  • Special requests and customer instructions.

Sensitive payment credentials, such as complete card numbers and security codes, are handled by authorised payment service providers and are not unnecessarily stored by the company.

How personal information is used

  • Preparing quotations and travel proposals.
  • Confirming and managing reservations.
  • Arranging accommodation, flights, transport and safari activities.
  • Communicating booking updates and travel information.
  • Processing payments and refunds.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Improving services and customer experiences.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.
  • Sending promotional materials where the customer has consented or another lawful basis applies.

Sharing information with third parties

Where necessary to arrange your trip, we may share information with:

  • Hotels, lodges and resorts.
  • Airlines and other transport providers.
  • Safari operators and tour guides.
  • National parks and conservation authorities.
  • Travel insurance providers.
  • Payment processors and financial institutions.
  • Technology and website service providers.

Children’s personal information

Where legally required, appropriate parental or guardian involvement and consent will be obtained. For children we collect age, names, passport details, and dietary and accessibility needs.

Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including hotel, airline and payment platforms. Therapy Tours & Travel is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.
