Safari to Shore: Amboseli, Taita Hills and Diani coast

Safari + beach9 days

Day by day

  1. Day 1 · Novotel WestlandsMeet our representative at the hotel or airport and transfer to the hotel. No meals included.
  2. Day 2 · Prara AmboseliDrive south from Nairobi, then east toward Mount Kilimanjaro through Maasai savanna country. Lunch at the lodge, then your first game drive in Amboseli.
  3. Day 3 · Saltlick Lodge / Taita Hills Safari Resort & SpaGame drive as you leave the park, then travel toward Voi past the volcanic scenery bordering Tsavo. Lunch at the lodge and an afternoon game drive.
  4. Day 4 · Leopard Beach ResortA last look at the elephants at the waterhole, then drive to the south coast using the Mombasa bypass bridge, arriving in under four hours. Afternoon at leisure.
  5. Day 5 · Leopard Beach ResortDay at leisure on half board with afternoon tea.
  6. Day 6 · Leopard Beach ResortDay at leisure on half board with afternoon tea.
  7. Day 7 · Leopard Beach ResortDay at leisure on half board with afternoon tea.
  8. Day 8 · Novotel WestlandsTransfer to Miritini station for the 15:00 train to Nairobi, arriving at 20:00, then transfer to the hotel.
  9. Day 9 · DepartAfter breakfast, transfer to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.

Good to know

  • Optional extra: one flight from Mombasa to Nairobi instead of the train.
  • Easter weekend: 26–29 Mar 2027. Christmas and New Year: 22 Dec 2027 – 2 Jan 2028.
  • Christmas/Easter/Valentine’s supplement at Prara Amboseli: US$25 adult, US$25 child. At Saltlick Safari: US$45 adult, US$30 child.
  • Saltlick Lodge requires a minimum 2-night stay between 22 Dec and 1 Jan.
  • Optional: one flight from Mombasa to Nairobi, US$125 per person.
