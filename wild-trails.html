Wild Trails: Amboseli, Naivasha and Maasai Mara

Three parks8 days

Day by day

  1. Day 1 · Eka HotelMeet our representative at the hotel or airport and transfer to the hotel. No meals included.
  2. Day 2 · Prara AmboseliDrive south and east toward Kilimanjaro, lunch at the lodge, then an afternoon game drive among the elephants of Amboseli.
  3. Day 3 · Prara AmboseliMorning and late-afternoon game drives, with the middle of the day to enjoy the camp. All meals included.
  4. Day 4 · Eka HotelReturn to Nairobi for lunch at Carnivore restaurant, then transfer to the hotel. No meals included.
  5. Day 5 · The Great Rift Valley LodgeDrive toward Lake Naivasha for lunch, then a one-hour boat ride to Crescent Island to see birdlife and hippos.
  6. Day 6 · Loyk Mara CampDrive along the Rift Valley floor via Mai Mahiu and Narok to the Maasai Mara. Lunch, then an afternoon game drive.
  7. Day 7 · Loyk Mara CampMorning and afternoon game drives, or an optional full-day drive with picnic lunch. All meals included.
  8. Day 8 · DepartAfter breakfast, drive to Nairobi and transfer to your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.

Good to know

  • Loyk Mara Camp includes a guided nature walk.
  • Easter weekend: 26–29 Mar 2027. Christmas and New Year: 22 Dec 2027 – 2 Jan 2028.
  • Christmas/Easter/Valentine’s supplement at Prara Amboseli: US$25 adult, US$25 child.
