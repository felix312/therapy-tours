Payment terms

After the agreement, we will send you the company account details for Therapy Tours & Travel, or you can pay through our online payment option.

The reservation payment is 40% of the total amount on your invoice (in US dollars, euros and/or pounds). If it is easier for you, you can pay for your safari in full. Payments by credit or debit card (American Express, Visa, Mastercard) attract a 5% transaction charge, while direct bank payment is free as of now.
